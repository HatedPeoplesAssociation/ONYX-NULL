# ONYX-NULL Privacy

## Purpose

ONYX-NULL aims to minimize unnecessary collection and exposure of user information.

Privacy is not the same thing as invisibility.

## Privacy Goals

The system should attempt to minimize:

* telemetry
* advertising identifiers
* unnecessary account dependencies
* background location collection
* unnecessary network connections
* persistent identifiers
* unnecessary cloud services

## Location

Location may potentially be inferred through:

* cellular infrastructure
* Wi-Fi
* Bluetooth
* GNSS
* IP addresses
* applications
* account activity
* metadata correlation

ONYX-NULL does not claim to make an active communications device impossible to locate.

## Communications

Encrypted communications can protect content.

They do not necessarily hide:

* who operates an account
* when communication occurs
* connection duration
* network addresses
* destination identifiers

## Privacy Controls

Future designs may include:

* hardware microphone isolation
* hardware camera isolation
* hardware radio isolation
* firewall controls
* VPN support
* minimal telemetry
* compartmentalized identities

## Philosophy

ONYX-NULL should describe exactly what information can still leak rather than pretending leakage does not exist.
