# ONYX-NULL Mechanical Design

This directory contains the mechanical design files, enclosure research, dimensional references, assembly notes, and exported models for ONYX-NULL.

Mechanical design is responsible for turning ONYX-NULL from a collection of electrical components into a usable handheld device.

# Mechanical Goals

ONYX-NULL mechanical design should prioritize:

- repairability
- serviceability
- structural strength
- reasonable weight
- thermal management
- accessible hardware switches
- replaceable components where practical
- accessible screws
- minimal unnecessary adhesive
- reproducible manufacturing
- documented dimensions
- accessible internal components
- safe battery mounting
- antenna clearance
- ergonomic handling

---

# Design Philosophy

ONYX-NULL should avoid becoming a sealed black box.

Where practical:

```text
SCREWS
  ↓
REMOVABLE BACK
  ↓
ACCESSIBLE INTERNALS
  ↓
REPLACEABLE COMPONENTS
```

should be preferred over:

```text
ADHESIVE
   ↓
SEALED ENCLOSURE
   ↓
DIFFICULT REPAIR
```

# Privacy Controls

Hardware privacy switches must remain physically accessible.

Possible controls include:

```text
[ WIFI ]
[ MIC  ]
[ CAM  ]
[ RADIO ]
```

The enclosure should make switch states easy to understand.

Switches should not be positioned where they can easily be activated accidentally.

---

# Serviceability

The device should eventually be designed so that common repairs do not require destructive disassembly.

Priority replacement components may include:

- battery
- display
- USB-C connector board
- speakers
- microphones
- buttons
- radio modules
- mainboard

---

# Fasteners

Where practical, use standardized screws.

Avoid unnecessary proprietary fasteners.

Document:

```text
Screw Type:
Thread:
Length:
Head Type:
Quantity:
Location:
Torque:
```

---

# Adhesives

Adhesives should be minimized where practical.

If adhesives are required:

- identify the adhesive
- document removal procedure
- document replacement procedure
- avoid permanent bonding where serviceability is desired

---

# Battery

The battery compartment must account for:

- expansion
- impact protection
- thermal isolation
- connector access
- replacement
- cable routing
- puncture prevention

Battery cells should never be mechanically compressed beyond manufacturer specifications.

---

# Display

Display integration must account for:

- active area
- bezel
- connector location
- FPC routing
- mounting pressure
- adhesive requirements
- replacement
- impact protection
- touch controller
- display thickness

---

# Antennas

Antenna placement must be coordinated with the radio design.

Mechanical design should avoid placing:

- large metal structures
- batteries
- shielding
- high-speed electronics

in locations that significantly degrade antenna performance.

RF decisions should be documented with the radio subsystem.

---

# Thermal Management

The enclosure must account for heat generated by:

- application processor
- PMIC
- charger
- battery
- Wi-Fi radio
- display
- regulators

Potential thermal components include:

- thermal pads
- heat spreaders
- internal frames
- graphite sheets
- ventilation where appropriate

The exterior surface temperature must remain safe for handheld use.

---

# Environmental Considerations

Future revisions may evaluate:

- dust resistance
- splash resistance
- water resistance
- impact resistance
- temperature range
- humidity
- UV exposure

ONYX-NULL should not claim an IP rating unless formally designed and tested for one.

---

# Prototype Philosophy

Early mechanical prototypes may be:

- oversized
- thick
- ugly
- 3D printed
- held together with screws
- full of unused internal space

That is acceptable.

The priority is:

```text
FUNCTION
   ↓
ACCESS
   ↓
TEST
   ↓
IMPROVE
   ↓
MINIATURIZE
```

# Mechanical Revisions

Suggested naming:

```text
ONX-ENC-P0

ONX-ENC-P1

ONX-ENC-R1-REV-A

ONX-ENC-R1-REV-B
```

Each revision should document:

- dimensions
- mass
- material
- manufacturing method
- compatible PCB revision
- compatible display
- compatible battery
- known issues
- test results
